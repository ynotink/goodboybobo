Rambo's Zoomies

Every match fills the Zoomies meter. When it's full, tap the button: Rambo races across the board and clears a whole row.

Tap pieces to fill the Zoomies meter
In the real game the meter fills from your normal matches, and the run would be a short animation of Rambo sprinting.

Pet Rambo for a life

Once an hour, rub Rambo with your finger. He loves it, and you get a free life.

Rub Rambo!
After the free life, it shows "Next pet in 59:59" so people come back.
